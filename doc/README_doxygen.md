\section intro_sec Introduction

This is the developer documentation of Tessera, a post-quantum UTXO cryptocurrency.
Tessera signs with ML-DSA-44 (FIPS 204) instead of an elliptic curve, hashes with
SHA3-256, and secures its chain with the SHA3-256d proof of work. It uses peer-to-peer
technology to operate with no central authority: managing transactions and issuing
money are carried out collectively by the network.

The software is released under the MIT license.

See https://github.com/MarcoFalke/tessera for further information about the project.
